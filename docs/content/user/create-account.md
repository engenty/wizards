engenty wizards is in a closed beta, so a new account needs an invitation code. “Get started” on
[engenty.ai](https://engenty.ai) takes your email and puts you on the list. The code comes by
email as soon as a seat is free. Once you have a code, the same button opens the sign-up.

## Sign up

1. Enter the code at [account.engenty.ai/sign-up](https://account.engenty.ai/sign-up). The link
   in the invitation email has it filled in.
2. Sign in with Google, GitHub or a link by email. There is no password.
3. The studio opens at [engenty.ai/studio](https://engenty.ai/studio).

Your account is a **team**: it holds your credits, your spaces and, on the Team plan, your
colleagues. It starts with credits, and some of them expire on a set date. The account pages at
[account.engenty.ai](https://account.engenty.ai) show them.

## The first visit

The studio asks four things, one per page. You can skip each one after the first and change it
later.

| Page | Asks | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| Who you are | “What’s your team called?”: your company, your club or just your name | The team’s name at account.engenty.ai |
| About you & your brand | “Where can the wizards read about you?”: your website. engenty reads your name, what you do, your logo and colours from it, or you write a line about yourself | [Space → Info & brand](./space.md#info--brand) |
| Knowledge | “What should the wizards know?”: documents the wizards may look things up in, such as price lists, your terms or an FAQ | [Space → Knowledge](./space.md#knowledge) |
| Your space | Nothing. It explains the space that holds all of this, “My wizards” (“Meine Wizards” in German) | – |

Then “Build your first wizard” opens the page for a new wizard: you describe a task in a
sentence, or start from a template. See [Build a wizard](./build-a-wizard.md).

## What the cloud does

- Wizards you build here run behind a link that works day and night, on the team’s credits.
- Share links, the tag for your website, QR codes and the channels of [Test and share](./test-and-share.md) work as they do on your computer.
- The team decides what the wizards run on: see [Models and account](./models.md).

## Also on your computer

The same account works on your own computer: you [install](./install.md) engenty wizards there
and sign in during the setup. It then runs on your Claude, ChatGPT, Google AI or Cursor
subscription, reads your files and browses the web for you. What you publish there runs in the
cloud too, behind the same links. In the cloud studio that space is marked “Edited on your
computer.” and you only look at it there: you change it on your computer.

On the studio’s start page, the “Also on your computer” card has “Email me the command”, which
sends you the install command.
