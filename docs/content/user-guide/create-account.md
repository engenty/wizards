engenty wizards is in a closed beta, so a new account needs an invitation code. “Get started” on
[engenty.ai](https://engenty.ai) takes your email and puts you on the list. The code comes by
email as soon as a seat is free. Once you have a code, the same button opens the sign-up.

## Sign up

1. Enter the code at [account.engenty.ai/sign-up](https://account.engenty.ai/sign-up). The link
   in the invitation email has it filled in.
2. Sign in with Google, GitHub or a link by email. There is no password.
3. The studio opens at [engenty.ai/studio](https://engenty.ai/studio).

Your account is an **organization**: it holds your credits and your spaces. It starts with credits, and some
of them expire on a set date. The account pages at [account.engenty.ai](https://account.engenty.ai)
show them.

## The first visit

The studio asks a few things in five steps. Everything after the first step can be skipped and
changed later.

| Page | Asks | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | “Build wizards for…” (myself, a club, a small business, a company), then “What’s your organization called?” (not asked for myself). With an email address of your organization’s own domain, the name its website gives is filled in | Your organization at account.engenty.ai |
| Brand | “Where can the wizards read about …?”: the website of your email address and what a web search finds for the name (website, profiles), to tick, and addresses of your own. engenty reads your name, what you do, your logo, colours and fonts, and the facts your imprint states; profiles are kept as facts. Nothing found: a few sentences about what you do, typed or spoken | [Sources → Info](./space.md#info) and [Brand](./space.md#brand) |
| Details | A few details that fit (size, sector, your role; a phone number for a company, optional) and “Wishes or questions”, typed or spoken: someone at engenty answers a question by email | Your organization at account.engenty.ai |
| Knowledge | “What should the wizards know?”: documents the wizards may look things up in, such as price lists, your terms or an FAQ | [Space → Knowledge](./space.md#knowledge) |
| Your space | Nothing. It explains the space that holds all of this, “My wizards” (“Meine Wizards” in German) | – |

Then “Build your first wizard” opens the page for a new wizard: you describe a task in a
sentence, or start from a template. See [Build a wizard](./build-a-wizard.md).

The questions about your organization are asked once, of the first admin who opens its studio.
In a second organization you are not greeted again and the space is not explained again: the
studio asks about that organization by its name and ends with its knowledge. Someone you invite,
and anyone who opens an organization that is set up already, sees one page instead: where they
are, who invited them, the spaces with their wizards and what their role may do.

## What the cloud does

- Wizards you build here run behind a link that works day and night, on the organization’s credits.
- Share links, the code for your website, QR codes and the chat of [Test and share](./test-and-share.md) work as they do on your computer.
- The organization decides what the wizards run on: see [Models and account](./models.md).

## Also on your computer

The same account works on your own computer: you [install](./install.md) engenty wizards there
and sign in during the setup. It then runs on your Claude, ChatGPT, Google AI or Cursor
subscription, reads your files and browses the web for you. What you publish there runs in the
cloud too, behind the same links. In the cloud studio that space is marked “Edited on your
computer.” and you only look at it there: you change it on your computer.

The welcome email has the install command.
