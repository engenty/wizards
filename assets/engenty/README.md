# engenty figures

Every flat engenty as a 512 × 512 PNG on a transparent ground: the ten shapes, and round, drop,
dome, flame, oval, sprout and tower also wearing Goose's flight helmet.

They are drawn from the flat figures in `apps/web/src/engenty/engenty.tsx` and `helmet.tsx`, at
the first frame of their idle motion, without the ground shadow.
